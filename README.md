# Prime_Mart_AI_Simulated_Data_Analysis
AI-generated retail sales dataset analysed using SQL Server and Excel to evaluate store performance, product sales, discounts, and profitability
# Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing the sales performance of three Prime Mart stores over a three-year period. The analysis examines the contribution of each store to overall sales growth and decline, considering factors such as total annual sales generated by each store, top- and bottom-selling products across the three stores based on the total revenue generated by each product, total discounts and average yearly discounts for each store, and the percentage contribution of each store to overall sales.

It also examines the yearly percentage performance of each store, the total profit generated by each location and the average yearly profit. Further investigation focuses on understanding why certain stores perform better in terms of sales while others experience declines in their sales performance.

# Project Objectives
- Investigate the sales performance of each store.
- Understand which products contribute most and least to sales revenue.
- Analyse the profit generated by each store.
- Examine sales trends across different months and years.
- Determine the yearly percentage change in sales performance across all three stores.

# Data Source 
The dataset was simulated and generated using OpenAI's ChatGPT AI system.

# Tools Used
- Microsoft SQL Server (SQL)
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power Query
# Data Cleaning
The data cleaning process involved examining the total number of rows and columns, column names, data types and missing values. Duplicate records were also investigated to determine their relevance to the analysis, after which relevant records were retained and unnecessary duplicates were removed.

No null or missing values, incorrect data types or impossible values were observed during the data inspection.

Additionally, the date column, originally recorded in the month/day/year format, was transformed using an Excel formula to extract the year and month separately. This was necessary to facilitate the monthly and yearly sales analysis.

# Business Insight
## Annual Sales Performance and Store Contribution

- The year 2025 recorded the highest sales revenue of ₦728,629,096 across the three years under review. This can be attributed, in part, to the fact that 2025 was the only year with a complete 12-month record of business transactions within the dataset. This provided a longer reporting period compared to 2024 and 2026 and was accompanied by higher recorded sales volume and customer patronage.-
- In 2024, Yaba store recorded the highest sales revenue of approximately ₦163 million, accounting for 35% of the total sales revenue for that year. Interestingly, Ikeja store, which ranked third with approximately ₦145 million in sales revenue in 2024, emerged as the highest-performing store in 2025. It generated approximately ₦252 million, representing about 35% of the year's total sales revenue and a 73.66% increase compared to its recorded revenue in 2024.
- Conversely, Yaba store moved from the leading position in 2024 to the lowest-performing store among the three locations in 2025, generating approximately ₦229 million, equivalent to 31.48% of the total sales revenue for the year. Despite this change in ranking, the store recorded an approximately 40% increase in sales revenue compared to the previous year.
-	In 2026, Lekki store emerged as the leading revenue contributor, generating approximately ₦95 million, which accounted for 35.79% of the total sales revenue recorded for the year.
-	Lekki store maintained a consistent second-place position in sales revenue between 2024 and 2025 before eventually emerging as the highest-performing store in 2026. The store also recorded a 60.03% increase in sales revenue between 2024 and 2025
-	It is important to note that the dataset contains different reporting periods across the three years. While 2025 covers a complete 12-month period (January–December), 2024 contains eight months of recorded transactions (May–December), while 2026 contains five months (January–May). These differences should be considered when interpreting the annual revenue comparisons.
## Product category Performance 
-	Electronics generated the highest sales revenue at approximately ₦200 million, followed by Beverages, which recorded approximately ₦194 million.
•	Groceries and Household products recorded the lowest sales revenue, generating approximately ₦170 million and ₦177 million, respectively.
•	This indicates that Electronics and Beverages contributed the most to overall sales revenue, while Groceries and Household products recorded comparatively lower revenue contributions.

## Profitability 
•	The profitability analysis revealed that Ikeja store generated the highest total profit of approximately ₦76 million, making it the leading store in terms of total profit generated among the three locations.

# Business recommendations
Based on the findings, the following recommendations are worth considering:

1. Investigate Ikeja's revenue growth: Management should examine the factors responsible for Ikeja's significant revenue increase between 2024 and 2025 to identify strategies that could be sustained or replicated across other locations.

2. Strengthen Yaba's sales performance: Although Yaba recorded an increase in revenue, its decline in store ranking suggests that management should investigate the factors behind its reduced relative contribution in 2025.

3. Maintain Lekki's growth momentum: Given its consistent second-place ranking in 2024 and 2025 and its leading position in 2026, management should identify the factors supporting its performance and explore opportunities to sustain this progress.

4. Review lower-performing product categories: Management should investigate the sales performance of Groceries and Household products to identify possible opportunities for improving demand, product availability and revenue generation.

5. Evaluate profitability beyond total profit: Comparing profit margins across the three stores will help management determine which location converts its revenue into profit most efficiently.
